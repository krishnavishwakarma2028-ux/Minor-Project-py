HOW IT WORKS

Four small steps.
Real numbers behind each.

01Find

OpenCV looks for the card’s four edges.

02Straighten

The photo is flattened to make text easier to read.

03Feature map

A small fixed-filter conv layer highlights edges/texture as a heatmap, and numpy measures sharpness, brightness, and edge density.

04Score

OCR keyword hits per class are turned into confidence percentages with a numpy softmax — the same last step a trained classifier uses.
